Privacy Policy

Resolve Within app and theresolvewithinproject.org · Effective date: October 2026

Resolve Within is a free self-help app for difficult moments. It offers breathing and grounding exercises, journaling, mood check-ins, daily messages, and a supportive community. The app is operated by The Resolve Within Project ("we," "us," "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Resolve Within app and this website collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • Resolve Within is free. The app has no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or donations.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • The breathing, grounding, panic, and crisis tools work without an account.
  • If you create an account, your journal, community posts, and preferences are stored on our servers so they can sync.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account & Data) or by email.

Information we collect

If you use the app without an account

Your onboarding choices, reminder settings, message preferences, favorites, and whether you've seen the in-app safety notice are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us unless you later create an account or sign in, at which point your onboarding choices are saved to your account. Whenever the app does connect to our server, the server receives basic technical information (see "Technical information" below).

Account information

  • Email sign-up: your email address, the name you enter, and your password. We store passwords only in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Continue with Google: Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture link (if you have one) with us. We also store the sign-in tokens Google issues so your Google login stays linked to your account. We never receive your Google password, and we don't access your Gmail, contacts, files, or other Google data.
  • Sign in with Apple (planned for iPhone): Apple shares your email address, or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email, and may share your name. If Apple doesn't share a name, your display name is "Member."
  • Account records: when your account was created, when you accepted the in-app safety notice, and whether your account has moderator access.

Content you create

  • Journal entries and mood check-ins: the mood you pick (cloudy, on edge, numb, heavy, or light) and any text you write. Your journal is private. Other users can't see it, and we use it only to show you your entries and your mood trends. Entries are limited to 5,000 characters.
  • Community posts: the text you post (up to 2,000 characters) and which community you posted in (Veteran or Healing Together). Posts are anonymous by default and show as "Anonymous." If you turn that off, your account name is shown. Either way, each post is linked to your account on our servers so we can moderate it.
  • Reactions: your likes, "encourage" reactions, and flags on posts.
  • Reports: when you report a post, we store the report, the type of report, the details you type, any username you include, and which account sent it. We don't tell the person you reported who reported them.
  • Breathing sessions and favorites: the type and length of breathing exercises you complete, and the exercises you mark as favorites.
  • Preferences: whether you describe yourself as a veteran, a civilian, or "prefer not to say"; which daily message streams you choose (mental health, veteran, faith); and your preferred reminder time. These are optional and are used only to tailor the daily messages and content you see. Your message style choices (tone and audience) are saved only on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by the app on your own device. We don't use a push notification service, and we don't collect a push token for your device. You can turn reminders off in the app (Profile → Notifications) or in your device settings.

Technical information

When the app connects to our server, the server receives your IP address, your device or browser type, and details of the request. Login sessions record the IP address and device type used to sign in. Our server logs record requests and account ID numbers so we can keep the service secure and fix problems. On your device, your sign-in token is kept in the phone's secure storage.

What we don't collect

  • Microphone: the app doesn't request or use your microphone. Audio in the app is playback only.
  • Location: the app doesn't access your device location. Crisis and support resources open as phone numbers or links you choose. If we ever add a location feature, it will ask your permission first, and we'll update this policy.
  • Camera, photos, contacts, and files: the app doesn't request access to any of these.
  • Advertising and tracking: the app contains no ads, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.
  • Payment information: the app has no payments of any kind.

Resolve Within is a self-help app. It is not a healthcare provider, and what you enter in it is not part of a medical record.

How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • create your account, sign you in, and keep your account secure;
  • save and sync your journal, mood trends, favorites, breathing history, and preferences across your devices;
  • show community posts and reactions;
  • review reports, moderate the community, and enforce our Terms of Use. Posts that receive several flags from members may be hidden automatically until they are reviewed;
  • choose which daily messages to show you, based on the message streams you pick;
  • keep the service running, fix bugs, and prevent abuse; and
  • meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or build advertising profiles. We don't use your journal or posts to train artificial intelligence (AI) models.

How we share information

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Service providers that run the app for us: Render (hosts our app server), Neon (hosts our database), and Expo (provides the tools we use to build and deliver the app). They handle data only to provide their services to us.
  • Sign-in providers you choose: Google and, when it's available, Apple. They handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies.
  • App stores: Google Play and the Apple App Store handle app downloads and updates under their own privacy policies.
  • Other users: your community posts and reactions are visible to other members. Posts show "Anonymous" unless you choose to show your name.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith that it's necessary to protect someone from serious harm.
  • If the project changes hands: if Resolve Within is transferred to a new operator, your information may transfer with it, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Your account data is stored on servers in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) between the app and our server. Passwords are hashed, and only a small number of authorized people can access the systems that run Resolve Within. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information.

How long we keep information

  • We keep your account data until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can delete individual journal entries in the app at any time.
  • Community posts stay visible until you delete your account or we remove them. To remove a specific post without deleting your account, email us.
  • When you delete your account in the app, your data is removed from our live database right away. Copies may remain in our hosting providers' backups and logs for up to about 30 days before they expire automatically.
  • Login sessions expire automatically.
  • If a moderator acted on your content, a short internal moderation log entry (the action taken, an internal ID number, and a reason) may be kept after your account is deleted. It doesn't include your email address or your journal.

See Delete your account for exactly what's deleted and how fast.

Your choices and rights

  • See and update your information: you can view your journal and change your preferences in the app. To get a copy of your data, email us.
  • Delete your information: delete your account in the app or request deletion by email.
  • Control reminders: turn them off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Post anonymously: community posts are anonymous unless you choose otherwise.

Depending on where you live (for example, California, the European Union, or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email us at contact@theresolvewithinproject.org. We'll respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Children

Resolve Within is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Our website

This website is hosted by Landingsite.ai, which may collect standard technical information about visits (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) to run the site and provide basic visitor statistics. If you enter your email address in a form on this site, we use it only to reply to you or send you updates about Resolve Within, and you can ask us to remove it at any time. If you use a Donate link on this website, you're taken to a payment page run by Stripe, which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your full card number. The app itself has no donations or payments.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If we make significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app or on this website.
