Support

Help with the Resolve Within app from The Resolve Within Project

Need help right now? This page and our email are not monitored around the clock. If you are in danger, call 911. In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7. Veterans can call 988 and press 1 or text 838255 (Veterans Crisis Line). Outside the U.S., find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Contact us

Email: contact@theresolvewithinproject.org

We're a small team and read every message. We usually reply within a few business days.

To help us help you, please include:

  • the type of phone you use (iPhone or Android) and, if you know it, the app version;
  • what you were trying to do and what happened instead; and
  • a screenshot, if it helps.

Please don't send your password or the contents of your journal.

Common questions

Is Resolve Within free?

Yes. Resolve Within is free to use, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads, or donations in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. The breathing, grounding, panic, and crisis resource tools work without an account. You need an account for the journal, the community, and syncing your data across devices. You can sign up with an email address and password or with Google. Sign in with Apple is planned for iPhone.

Where can I get the app?

Resolve Within is coming soon to Google Play and the Apple App Store.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account & Data. You can also ask us to delete it by email. See Delete your account for step-by-step instructions and what gets deleted.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address on your account and we'll help you get back in.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Profile → Notifications in the app. You can also turn off notifications for Resolve Within in your phone's settings.

How do I change my daily messages?

Go to Profile → Message Preferences to choose the tone (calm, encouraging, or direct) and audience (general or veteran-aware) of your daily messages.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Other users can't see your journal. Community posts are anonymous by default unless you choose to show your name. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a post or a member?

Tap the flag icon on the post and choose Report Content or User or Report Safety Concern. You can also email us with details. Reports are reviewed when possible and are not an emergency service. See our Terms of Use for the community rules.

I have an idea or found a bug.

We'd love to hear it. Email us at contact@theresolvewithinproject.org.
