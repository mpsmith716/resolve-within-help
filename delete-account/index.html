Delete your Resolve Within account and data

Resolve Within app by The Resolve Within Project · Effective date: October 2026

You can permanently delete your Resolve Within account and the data linked to it at any time. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email if you no longer have the app.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Use one of the options below.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Resolve Within and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Under Privacy, tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Review the list of data that will be deleted, then tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. If you signed up with an email address and password, enter your password. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you don't need a password.
  6. Tap Yes, Delete My Account.

Your account is deleted right away. The app signs you out and returns to the sign-in screen. If the app shows "Deletion Failed," your account was not deleted. Check your connection (and your password, if you use one) and try again.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (no app needed)

Email contact@theresolvewithinproject.org with the subject "Delete my Resolve Within account." Please:

  • send it from the email address on your Resolve Within account, if you can;
  • tell us how you sign in (email and password, Google, or Apple); and
  • if you used Apple's Hide My Email, include the private relay address (it ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com). You can find it in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from the account's email address before we delete anything. We'll email you when your account has been deleted. We complete email requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Delete only some of your data

  • Journal entries: you can delete any entry yourself in the app.
  • A specific community post: email us with the post's text and the date you posted it, and we'll remove it without deleting your account.
  • Your email on our website's sign-up list: ask us in your email and we'll remove it.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

  • your account and sign-in details: email address, name, password (stored in hashed form), any linked Google or Apple sign-in, and all login sessions;
  • your journal entries and mood check-ins;
  • your community posts, likes, "encourage" reactions, and flags, along with any reports and reactions attached to your posts;
  • reports you submitted about other posts;
  • your breathing exercise history and favorites;
  • your preferences: veteran or civilian status, daily message streams, reminder time, and when you accepted the in-app safety notice; and
  • on the phone you delete from: your saved settings, favorites, reminder settings, scheduled reminders, and sign-in token.

After deletion, you can no longer sign in with that account. If you sign up again, you start over with a new, empty account. This can't be undone.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Backups and server logs: copies of deleted data may remain in our hosting providers' backups and logs for up to about 30 days, until they expire automatically.
  • Moderation log entries: if a moderator acted on your content, a short internal log entry (the action taken, an internal ID number, and a reason) may be kept to keep moderation accountable. It doesn't include your email address or your journal.
  • Legal requirements: we may keep specific information for longer if the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid legal request.
  • Other phones: settings saved on any other phone where you used the app stay on that phone until you uninstall the app. Your deleted account won't work there.
  • Copies made by others: we can't delete copies of public posts that other people saved or screenshotted.

How fast

  • In the app: immediately.
  • By email: within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • Backup and log copies: expire automatically within about 30 days.
