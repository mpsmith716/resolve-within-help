5. Community rules

The community spaces (Veteran and Healing Together) are for supportive, respectful peer connection. When you post or react, you agree to these rules:

  • Be respectful. Treat others with dignity. Share support, encouragement, and lived experience without putting others down.
  • No harassment. Don't bully, stalk, intimidate, or repeatedly target anyone. Unwanted contact and personal attacks are not allowed.
  • No hate or threats. Content that attacks people based on who they are (such as race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or veteran status), or that threatens violence or harm, is not allowed.
  • No dangerous content. Don't post content that promotes or encourages self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or dangerous or illegal activity, and don't share methods of self-harm. If you are struggling, please reach out to 988 or local emergency services. The community is not a crisis hotline.
  • Share experience, not professional advice. Don't present yourself as a medical or mental health professional, and don't tell others to stop treatment or medication.
  • Protect privacy. Don't share anyone else's private information, such as phone numbers, addresses, or medical details, without their permission. Think carefully before sharing details that could identify you.
  • No sexual or illegal content. No sexually explicit content, and nothing that sexualizes minors. No content that breaks the law or infringes someone else's rights.
  • No spam or impersonation. Don't post ads, scams, repetitive posts, or unrelated promotions, and don't pretend to be someone else.

6. Reporting

You can also email us at contact@theresolvewithinproject.org. We review reports when possible and don't tell the reported person who reported them. Posts that receive several flags from members may be hidden automatically until they are reviewed.

Reporting is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or 988 right away.
